Where the numbers come from

Ledgerline connects to the bank over the same open-banking feed your accountant already uses, and reads invoices out of whatever you send them from.

Bank feed
Read-only, refreshed at 6am and again at noon.
Invoices
Imported from Moloni, InvoiceXpress, or a folder of PDFs.
Credit notes
Matched to the invoice they cancel, so a refund stops reading as a gap.
Bank charges
Recognised and set aside, instead of counted as an unexplained line.
Your notes
Whatever you wrote against a line last month is still there this month.

Two hundred invoices a month or fewer.

Above that, the reconciliation stops being the hard part and the workflow does. We say so here rather than after you sign up.

If your firm is past it, the honest answer is that this is not the tool, and we would rather you found that out on a Tuesday than in your third month.
