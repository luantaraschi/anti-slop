# 🚀 Tideline 2.3: Taking Your Tidal Planning To The Next Level

We're thrilled to announce Tideline 2.3 — a release that marks a pivotal moment
in our journey toward becoming the most powerful tidal planning platform for
professional guides. This update underscores our commitment to accuracy,
reliability, and confidence on the water.

Let's dive in.

## ✨ What's New

- **Offline Charts:** Offline charts are now available — ensuring you stay
  informed even when connectivity is limited.
- **Improved Accuracy:** Accuracy has been significantly enhanced — delivering
  more reliable predictions.
- **Performance:** Performance has been optimized across the board — resulting
  in a seamless experience.

Tideline has always been about more than just tide tables — it's about
**confidence on the water**. At its core, what really matters is trust. In order
to deliver on that promise, our team has been working tirelessly to refine the
core prediction engine, leveraging a **robust new model** that serves as the
foundation for everything that follows.

It is important to note that the new engine could potentially reduce planning
errors for most users — we believe this will likely become the **standard
workflow** for professional guides within the next season.

You need to understand that spring tides are where most planning errors happen.
Think of it like a compounding error — small offsets at slack water cascade into
significant scheduling problems — and the team calls it “slack drift.”

## 🔧 Under The Hood

There's a lot happening beneath the surface.

Due to the fact that iOS 15 no longer receives security updates, support has been
dropped. This was a difficult decision — but one that allows us to focus on
delivering a truly cutting-edge experience, fostering the kind of reliability our
users deserve.
